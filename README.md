# Carrom

A real-size 3D carrom board you can play in the browser, built with three.js.

Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder with any static server, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo).

## The scene
- Tournament-size board: 74 × 74 cm playing surface, 4.45 cm pockets, regulation baselines, arrows and centre rosette
- Polished dark jade playing surface with live mirror reflections, procedural nephrite texture
- Smoky translucent orange frame on a lacquered birch table with a few dark grain lines
- Hollow ring striker, 19 coins in the standard break formation
- Room with sky-blue panel walls, grey tiled floor, orange disc uplights and hanging red resin balls

## Controls
**Viewing** – drag sideways to orbit, drag up/down to change eye height, pinch or scroll to zoom. Sliders and preset views are in the bottom tray. The camera always looks at the centre of the board.

**Playing** – tap *Arm striker*. The view freezes and the controls hide. Touch the striker and flick: the direction and speed of your flick set the shot. Drag anywhere else to turn the view for aiming. Double-tap (or double-click, or Esc) to disarm. After each shot the striker returns to the baseline once everything stops.
